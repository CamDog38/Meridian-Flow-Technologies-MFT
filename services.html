Services

Strong technology deserves a clear route to market

MFT provides international manufacturers with a clear route into the Middle East and Africa's energy markets, from initial market assessment and technology acceptance through to approved vendor status, strategic account development and long-term commercial growth.

Service 1

Market Intelligence and Technology Acceptance

Understand the opportunity before committing resources.

We conduct targeted market studies to assess commercial potential, technology suitability, competitive positioning and barriers to entry. Our assessments can include:

  • Market size, customer demand and project opportunities.
  • Competitor analysis and existing approved suppliers.
  • Technical specifications and product acceptance requirements.
  • Local-content policies and localisation considerations.
  • A structured Market Feasibility and Technology Acceptance Report, including recommendations and a practical market-entry roadmap.
Service 2

Vendor Qualification and Product Approval

From initial engagement to approved vendor status.

We coordinate the complete vendor qualification process with National Oil Companies (NOCs), International Oil Companies (IOCs), EPC contractors and major operators. Our services include:

  • Vendor registration and prequalification.
  • Technical documentation and qualification submissions.
  • Engagement with engineering, procurement and approval authorities.
  • Identification and resolution of qualification gaps.
  • Coordination of technical evaluations, audits and product acceptance.
  • Management of the approval process through to Approved Vendor List (AVL) inclusion.

Final technical acceptance and vendor approval remain subject to each customer's requirements and decisions.

Service 3

Channel Development and Management

Establish the right commercial presence.

We develop and manage effective routes to market, connecting manufacturers with qualified representatives, distributors, EPC contractors and local partners. Our support includes:

  • Channel strategy and partner identification.
  • Distributor and representative evaluation.
  • Commercial agreement development.
  • Channel performance management.
  • Coordination of local service and support capabilities.
Service 4

Strategic Account Leadership and Project Pursuit

Transform vendor approval into long-term business.

Achieving approval is only the beginning. MFT develops and manages strategic customer relationships to convert technical acceptance into project opportunities, repeat orders and a growing installed base. Our services include:

  • Strategic account planning and customer engagement.
  • Relationship development with technical and commercial decision-makers.
  • Project identification and opportunity development.
  • Coordination of specifications, tenders and commercial opportunities.
  • Installed-base development, aftermarket and expansion opportunities.
Service 5

Advisory and Consultancy Services

Specialist expertise, tailored to your requirements.

Manufacturers can engage MFT for individual assignments, strategic advice or ongoing commercial support without committing to a full market-entry programme. Consultancy services cover market feasibility, technology positioning, vendor qualification strategies, channel development and strategic account management.

Whether supporting a specific challenge or developing a complete regional growth strategy, MFT provides independent, commercially focused guidance backed by extensive industry experience.

Flexible engagement models

From a single assessment to a long-term commercial partnership. MFT offers flexible engagement arrangements aligned with each manufacturer's objectives, including project-based consultancy, defined qualification programmes, ongoing market representation and strategic account management.
